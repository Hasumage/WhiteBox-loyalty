# Nearloy Hunt Product Concept

## Thesis

Nearloy Hunt should make Nearloy useful before companies join the platform.

The user plays a local blogger/scout. They visit real places, publish posts in Nearloy, receive Nearloy likes, earn game currency and collect cute creature cards. The game creates local content and demand first; companies can later claim places, sponsor boxes, run quests and convert attention into loyalty mechanics.

This follows the same strategic pattern as a product expanding into a culturally stronger branch: Nearloy starts as loyalty infrastructure, but NH can become a local discovery and collecting habit.

## Core Loop

1. A user visits or selects a real place.
2. The user creates a post with text, optional place name, checked address or attached geolocation, categories, rating and up to 3 photos.
3. Other users react with Nearloy likes.
4. The server converts trusted social activity into `NearCoin`.
5. NearCoin opens boxes or upgrades cards.
6. Boxes drop cute collectible monsters with rarity, element, stats and level.
7. Missions and streaks guide the user back into posting, reacting and discovering places.

## Product Rules

- Posts are the primary game action.
- GPS is used as context and proof, not as a rigid map-only gate.
- Likes are social signal, not direct money.
- Rewards must be server-authoritative and abuse-resistant.
- Characters are stylized cute monsters, not humans or realistic tactical characters.
- Cards are web2-first digital collectibles. NFT minting is optional and belongs to a later phase.
- Companies are not required at launch. User-created places and discovery posts can bootstrap the content graph.

## Why It Helps Nearloy

- Gives users a reason to open Nearloy even without partner companies nearby.
- Produces place-level content that later helps company acquisition.
- Builds local rankings and discovery surfaces from real user behavior.
- Turns likes into a game economy without promising financial rewards.
- Creates shareable assets: posts, cards, boxes, missions and local achievements.

## Launch Positioning

The first version should feel like:

> A local blogging game inside Nearloy: post about places, earn NearCoin, open boxes, collect city creatures.

Avoid early positioning as:

- A crypto/NFT product.
- A play-to-earn game.
- A Pokemon clone.
- A company loyalty feature that only works after partners sign up.

## Content And Rights

NH depends on posts, photos, place metadata and derived game content. Product/legal docs must clearly grant Nearloy a broad, non-exclusive UGC license to store, display, promote, adapt and reuse posts inside Nearloy surfaces and marketing.

The legal flow should also cover:

- Geolocation data.
- Photo and media storage.
- Moderation and takedowns.
- User reports.
- Public sharing of posts/cards.
- Future sponsored quests and Russian internet ad labeling requirements when applicable.
